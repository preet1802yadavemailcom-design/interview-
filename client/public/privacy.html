InterviewCapture is designed for local-first recording. The video recording created by the host is not uploaded to InterviewCapture or stored in Supabase by this app.

Information used to run a room

When you create or join a room, the app uses a temporary anonymous Supabase session. The room service stores a room code, a display name, a host/guest role, a user identifier, and room timing information so the two participants can find each other and connect. Rooms expire after four hours. When a participant leaves normally, their room membership is removed; an interrupted browser session may remain until expiry.

Audio, video, and screen capture

Your browser asks for camera and microphone permission before joining. Live audio and video are sent peer-to-peer using WebRTC. The host can start recording only after the host and guest each confirm consent inside the app. The recording is composed in the host's browser and remains in browser memory until the host saves it to their device.

If the host selects screen/window/tab capture, that screen video is included only in the host's local recording in this MVP. It is not transmitted to the guest. Screen/system audio is not captured; both participants' microphone audio is mixed into the recording.

Service providers

Supabase provides anonymous authentication, room membership functions, and Realtime signaling/presence. Cloudflare serves the web application. These providers may process technical data as part of operating their services under their own terms and privacy notices. InterviewCapture does not intentionally upload recorded media to Supabase Storage.

Cookies and analytics

The app does not include advertising or third-party analytics code in this starter. Supabase authentication stores a browser session so a participant can remain associated with their room.

Your choices

You can deny camera/microphone permissions, decline recording consent, leave a room, or choose not to save a recording. Use the browser's site settings to revoke permissions or clear the local session.

Important

This notice describes the behavior of the included MVP code. Before a public launch, the project owner should add a support contact, review this notice for the final deployed configuration, and obtain any required legal review. Recording laws vary by location; the host remains responsible for explaining the recording and complying with applicable requirements.

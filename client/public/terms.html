TERMS OF USE

Use it thoughtfully.

Last updated: November 16, 2026

By creating or joining an InterviewCapture room, you agree to use the service lawfully and respectfully.

Respect and consent

Do not record a person without informing them and obtaining their consent. Both host and guest must confirm recording consent in the app before recording starts. The host is responsible for explaining what will be captured, why it is being recorded, and how the resulting file will be used.

Permitted use

Use InterviewCapture only for lawful interviews, research, education, hiring, and other legitimate conversations. Do not use it to harass, impersonate, surveil, or violate another person's privacy or intellectual-property rights.

Recording and storage

In this MVP, recordings are created in the host's browser and saved by the host to their own device. InterviewCapture does not upload or back up the recording. The host is responsible for protecting, storing, sharing, and deleting the saved file.

Availability and compatibility

The service is provided as an evolving MVP. Browser support, network conditions, device permissions, and firewalls can affect calling and recording. A TURN relay may be required on some networks. Do not rely on the service as the only copy of a critical interview; verify each recording immediately after saving it.

No warranty

The application is provided as-is, without a guarantee of uninterrupted availability, successful connection, recording quality, or suitability for a particular purpose. Back up important recordings and test your workflow before using it for important sessions.

Changes

These terms may be updated as the product evolves. The project owner should review this page and add a support contact and any jurisdiction-specific terms before a public launch.
